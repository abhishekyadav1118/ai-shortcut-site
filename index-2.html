What we make

The AI Shortcut is a YouTube channel that explains new AI tools in plain English. Each video covers what a tool does, who it is for, how to try it, and where it falls short.

  • Simple breakdowns of new AI tools and updates
  • Step-by-step guides you can follow the same day
  • Honest pros, cons, and who should skip a tool

To watch, search for The AI Shortcut on YouTube.

About the app

"The AI Shortcut Autopilot" is a private tool run by the channel owner. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload finished videos, thumbnails, and descriptions to the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from viewers.
