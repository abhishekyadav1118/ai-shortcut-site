Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

This policy applies to "The AI Shortcut Autopilot" (the "App"), a private tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel "The AI Shortcut" to publish videos to that channel.

Who uses the App

The App is used only by the channel owner. It is not offered to the public, and viewers of the channel do not sign in to it or interact with it.

What the App accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the App asks for permission to manage videos on the owner's own YouTube channel. It uses this permission only to:

  • upload videos to the owner's channel,
  • set video titles, descriptions, tags, and thumbnails, and
  • schedule when those videos are published.

What we store

The App stores an access token that Google issues after the owner signs in. This token is kept as an encrypted secret in the owner's private code repository and is never published or shown to anyone else. The App does not collect, store, or process personal data about viewers, subscribers, or any other person.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, or share Google user data with third parties. We do not use Google user data for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The App also uses YouTube API Services and follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

The owner can remove the App's access at any time from the Google Account permissions page at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Once access is removed, the App can no longer act on the channel.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

To ask a question about this policy, contact us through the "The AI Shortcut" channel on YouTube.
